# B2B Appointment Setting Workflow

## 1. Identify the Target Prospect

Confirm that the prospect matches the campaign criteria:

- Correct industry
- Appropriate company size
- Relevant location
- Suitable decision-maker
- Potential need for the offered service

## 2. Make the Initial Contact

The purpose of the first call is to:

- Introduce yourself professionally
- Explain the reason for contacting the prospect
- Present a concise value statement
- Identify whether the prospect has a relevant need

## 3. Qualify the Prospect

Ask clear and respectful questions:

- How do you currently manage this area of your business?
- Are you currently using a similar service?
- What challenges are you experiencing?
- Who is involved in making this decision?
- Would a short discussion with a specialist be useful?

## 4. Present the Appointment

If the prospect shows interest:

- Explain the purpose of the meeting
- Confirm the expected duration
- Offer suitable date and time options
- Confirm the correct email address
- Send or arrange the meeting invitation according to the client’s process

## 5. Confirm the Appointment

Before ending the call, confirm:

- Prospect’s name
- Company name
- Meeting date
- Meeting time and time zone
- Contact email
- Meeting method
- Main discussion topic

## 6. Record the Appointment

Update the CRM or tracking sheet with:

- Prospect details
- Qualification notes
- Appointment date and time
- Decision-maker status
- Key business needs
- Follow-up requirements
- Call outcome

## 7. Follow-Up

Before the appointment:

- Send a confirmation message if required
- Share relevant meeting details
- Inform the client or sales representative
- Update the appointment status

## 8. Professional Standards

- Never promise results that cannot be guaranteed.
- Never pressure a prospect into accepting an appointment.
- Respect requests not to be contacted.
- Record information accurately.
- Protect confidential prospect and client information.
- Report booked appointments honestly.
